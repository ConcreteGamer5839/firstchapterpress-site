Reading list

If you loved The Thursday Murder Club

Older sleuths, sharp tongues, and people underestimating them at their peril. What to read next.

Chosen by Claire Willowbrook · Updated October 2026

The Thursday Murder Club proved what cozy readers already knew: the most dangerous person in the room is often the one everyone has stopped watching. If you finished it wanting more of that feeling, start here.

1

The Man Who Died Twice

Richard Osman · Thursday Murder Club, book 2

The obvious one first: if you've only read the first, the gang is back, and Elizabeth's past arrives with it. The series keeps getting warmer.

Find it on Amazon →
2

The Unexpected Mrs. Pollifax

Dorothy Gilman · Mrs. Pollifax, book 1

A widow from New Jersey volunteers for the CIA and, through a mix-up, gets sent on a real job. Older heroine, a lot of nerve, a great deal of fun.

Find it on Amazon →
3

The Windsor Knot

S.J. Bennett · Her Majesty the Queen Investigates, book 1

Queen Elizabeth II quietly solves a death at Windsor Castle while letting everyone else take the credit. Clever premise, and it works.

Find it on Amazon →
4

Agatha Raisin and the Quiche of Death

M.C. Beaton · Agatha Raisin, book 1

Early retirement, a Cotswolds cottage, and a woman who refuses to fade into the background. Agatha is the patron saint of underestimated middle age.

Find it on Amazon →

Our book

Tales from the Twisted Leaf

Claire Willowbrook · three mysteries in one volume

Ours. Not retirees, but a woman who walked away from a big career to start over in a small harbor town, and keeps seeing what the locals talk themselves out of. Short, quick reads. Free in Kindle Unlimited.

See it on Amazon →
6

A Murder Is Announced

Agatha Christie · Miss Marple

A notice in the village paper announces a murder, time and place included, and the neighbors turn up expecting a party game. Miss Marple at her sharpest.
