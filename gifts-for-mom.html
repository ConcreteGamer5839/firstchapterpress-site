How to send a Kindle book as a gift

  1. Open the book's Kindle page on Amazon (any "Kindle" link above).
  2. Choose Buy for others on the right side of the page.
  3. Enter her email address, add a short message, and pick the day you want it delivered. Christmas morning works.
  4. Place the order. She gets an email with a link and reads it on a Kindle or in the free Kindle app.

Amazon only sends Kindle gifts to someone in the same country as you. If you'd rather hand it over yourself, you can have the gift email sent to you instead and forward it.
