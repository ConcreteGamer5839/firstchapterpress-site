About

A small press for small towns

First Chapter Press is a tiny independent imprint. We publish cozy mysteries set in harbor towns on the Washington coast, and we keep this site for everybody who reads the same kind of book we like to write.

It's run by Claire Willowbrook, author of the Twisted Leaf and Foxglove Nursery mysteries. The reading lists are books we'd recommend to a friend. Our own books are always marked as ours.
