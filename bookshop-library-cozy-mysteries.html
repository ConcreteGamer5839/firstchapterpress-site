Reading list

Bookshop and library cozy mysteries

Six cozy mysteries set among the shelves, from a New Hampshire book town to a library inside a lighthouse.

Chosen by Claire Willowbrook · Updated November 2026

People who sell books and lend them spend their days watching what other people read, and they tend to notice when something is off. These are cozy mysteries set in bookshops and libraries, one of them inside a lighthouse. Each starts a series, so if one suits you there's more behind it.

1

Murder Is Binding

Lorna Barrett · Booktown Mysteries, book 1

After her divorce, Tricia Miles opens Haven't Got a Clue, a mystery bookstore in Stoneham, New Hampshire, a small town counting on its bookshops to bring in the tour buses. Not every local is happy about the newcomers, so when Tricia finds an unpleasant neighbor dead, the police aren't in a hurry to help her. Her sister Angelica turns up soon after and joins in.

2

Books Can Be Deceiving

Jenn McKinlay · Library Lover's Mysteries, book 1

Lindsey is settling in as director of the Briar Creek Public Library. Her friend Beth hopes a visiting New York editor will look at the children's book she has written, but Beth's boyfriend, a famous author, gets in the way. After Beth and Lindsey confront him, he is found murdered.

Find it on Amazon →
3

Homicide in Hardcover

Kate Carlisle · Bibliophile Mysteries, book 1

Brooklyn Wainwright restores rare books in San Francisco. She finds her old mentor, Abraham, dying, and his last words to her are “Remember the devil.” He leaves her the job of finishing his restoration of a supposedly cursed copy of Goethe's Faust. A good pick if you like old paper and bindings as much as the mystery.

Find it on Amazon →
4

A Murder for the Books

Victoria Gilbert · Blue Ridge Library Mysteries, book 1

Amy Webber has moved into her family's old house in Taylorsford, Virginia, and become the town librarian. Her new neighbor asks her to help clear the name of a woman acquitted of poisoning her novelist husband long ago, and then a body turns up in the library's archive building. An old case and a new one, wound together.

Find it on Amazon →
5

By Book or By Crook

Eva Gates · Lighthouse Library Mysteries, book 1

Lucy Richardson leaves her job with rare books at the Harvard library for a library inside a lighthouse on Bodie Island, in North Carolina's Outer Banks. A first edition of a Jane Austen novel goes missing, the chair of the library board is murdered, and Lucy is in the middle of both.

Find it on Amazon →
6

Death on Demand

Carolyn G. Hart · Death on Demand Mysteries, book 1 (1987)

Annie Laurance owns Death on Demand, a mystery bookstore on Broward's Rock, an island off South Carolina. An author is killed during the Sunday night meeting of local mystery writers at her shop, and Annie is the main police suspect. The grandmother of the bookshop cozy, and the series runs a long way.

Claire Willowbrook · The Foxglove Nursery Mysteries, book 1: A Body in the Bee Balm

Marta Pettibone spent thirty-one years as a hospice nurse.
