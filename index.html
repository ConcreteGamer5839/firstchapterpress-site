Tales from the Twisted Leaf

by Claire Willowbrook

Emma Calloway gave up a Seattle law career for a weathered blue tea shop on Harbor Street in Harrow's Cove, Washington. Over one year, from a crisp October morning to the first blue light of spring, she keeps noticing the things everyone else misses. A dead antique dealer the police say died of natural causes. A winter storm and a missing set of historical documents. A stranger with a story that ties the whole autumn together.

Three short mysteries, best read in order, with a pot of something hot.

Why this exists

I started keeping a list of cozy mysteries the way some people keep recipe cards: the ones I'd hand to a friend, the ones that were better than their covers, the ones that turned up free on Kindle Unlimited when I wasn't looking. Eventually the list got long enough that it seemed selfish not to share it.

So that's what this is. A short letter once a week, a few reading lists, and our own books when there's a new one. No pop-ups, no countdown timers, and I won't pretend a book is good because someone paid for it to be here. If a pick is sponsored, it says so.

Claire

Claire Willowbrook, First Chapter Press
