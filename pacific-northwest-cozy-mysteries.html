Reading list

Small-town Pacific Northwest mysteries

Six cozy mysteries set in Northwest towns, from a mountain newspaper office to a cat sanctuary in the San Juan Islands.

Chosen by Claire Willowbrook · Updated November 2026

Our own books are set in harbor towns on the Washington coast, so this list is close to home. The Northwest has plenty of small towns where everyone knows your car, and enough rain to keep people indoors talking. Each pick here starts a series, so if one suits you there's more behind it.

1

The Alpine Advocate

Mary Daheim · the Alpine mysteries, book 1 (1992)

Emma has bought the weekly paper in Alpine, a small town in the Cascade foothills of Washington, and she is hoping for a big story. She gets one when the grandson of a wealthy local family is murdered, and his relatives are in no hurry to answer her questions. The series runs long, and the small-paper newsroom is half the fun.

Find it on Amazon →
2

Miss Zukas and the Library Murders

Jo Dereske · Miss Zukas Mysteries, book 1 (1994)

Helma Zukas is the librarian in Bellehaven, Washington, and she likes her rules followed and her shelves in order. When murder turns up at her library, she treats it the way she treats any question at the desk and goes looking for the answer.

Find it on Amazon →
3

Romeow and Juliet

Kathi Daley · Whales and Tails Mysteries, book 1

Cait Hart helps run her aunt's cat sanctuary on Madrona Island, an invented island in Washington's San Juans, and co-owns a bookstore and coffee bar with a cat lounge. She and her dog Max find a council member dead in an old fish cannery. Meanwhile a stray cat named Romeo will not stop visiting her show cat, Juliet.

Find it on Amazon →
4

Death on Tap

Ellie Alexander · Sloan Krause Mysteries, book 1 (2017)

Sloan Krause brews beer in Leavenworth, the Bavarian-style village in the Washington Cascades. After she catches her husband cheating, she leaves the family brewhaus for a tiny start-up brewery. Then a body turns up, and her husband is the prime suspect.

Find it on Amazon →
5

Murder at Last Chance Cove

Kim Griswell · Pacific Northwest Cozy Mystery, book 1 (2025)

Saffi Graywood is a writer spending the summer in an RV park on the Oregon coast to finish her latest book. She finds the park manager's body hidden in the driftwood, in a town where gossip moves faster than the tide. The newest book on this list.

Find it on Amazon →
6

Who Moved My Goat Cheese?

Lynn Cahoon · Farm-to-Fork Mysteries, book 1 (2018)

A little inland, in River Vista, Idaho. Angie Turner is three weeks from opening a farm-to-table restaurant in her old hometown when Old Man Moss, the cranky goat farmer she hopes to buy cheese from, is murdered. She ends up looking after his goats while she works out who did it.

A plant nursery above a Washington harbor

Claire Willowbrook · The Foxglove Nursery Mysteries, book 1: A Body in the Bee Balm

Marta Pettibone spent thirty-one years as a hospice nurse in Tacoma. Her aunt leaves her two acres of plant nursery in Gullwater, Washington, along with a goose named Custis, and then a neighbor turns up dead in the display garden. The county calls it his heart; Marta thinks the timing is wrong. Books two and three, The Wolfsbane Window and The Hemlock Bed, are out too. Free to read in Kindle Unlimited.
